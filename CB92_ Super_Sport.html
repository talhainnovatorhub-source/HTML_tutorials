1959 Benly CB92 Super Sport


The first mass production model to adopt the ”CB” monicker, this supersport model was powered by a 4-stroke
OHC 2-cylinder 125cc engine.
In 1959, Hajime Kitano rode his CB92 to victory in the Mount Asama Volcano Race, cementing its reputation as
a high-performance machine.
This was beginning of the history of CB, Honda's 4-stroke engine road sport models.
